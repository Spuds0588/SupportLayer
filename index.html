One line of code, and your app
has real support built in.

Paste a script tag and support is live. Your users ask for help from the exact screen they're stuck on — a report, a live chat, or a screen session your agent can point and draw on. Every request lands on the webhook you already run, so it plugs into whatever you support with today. No backend, no second app to host.

    1. A normal checkout — with one script tag on it. Nothing else to install.
    2. The payment fails. They ask for help without leaving the page they're on.
    3. They describe it. Redaction runs on the page, before anything is captured or sent.
    4. Sending it starts the screen share, and it stays on for the whole session — ending the session is what stops it.

    Getting started

    It's a script tag. That really is the whole setup.

    Add one line

    <script
    src="/supportlayer.js"
    data-webhook="https://api.domain.com/webhook"
    data-mode="chat"
    data-theme="#14b8a6"
    data-blur-selectors=".sensitive-data, .balance"
    data-fields='[{"name":"issue","type":"textarea",
                  "label":"What went wrong?"}]'
></script>

    Reports POST straight to a webhook you already own. Point data-mode at none for async bug reports, at chat for a live session they type through, or at video for a two-way call. How they reach you is your call on that tag — not a setting the user can flip. Either live mode carries their screen with it, and it stays shared until the session ends.

    Getting your agent in

    Your webhook hands you back a link that is the customer's own page plus two parameters. Open it and you're in the session — no second app, no portal, and no auth of its own to build.

    // what live_session_url looks like
https://app.domain.com/checkout
   ?sl_role=agent&peer=sl-7f3a91

    Your agent gets the customer's screen with a floating dock for pointing, drawing and handing over text — closer to a video call than a dashboard. The shared surface keeps the customer's viewport ratio and scales to fit the agent window, so those tools stay aligned. And whatever already protects /checkout protects this, because it is /checkout.

    That dock and the chat are the entire agent UI. No ticket list, no metrics pane, no separate tool to learn.

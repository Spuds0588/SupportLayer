See exactly what your users see.
One script tag, no backend.

SupportLayer captures a one-frame snapshot, redacts the sensitive parts before capture, and POSTs the report straight to your webhook. When a report isn't enough, your support agent opens the same URL with role parameters and lands in a full-screen agent view: the customer's screen, the chat, and a floating dock of annotation tools — without a relay server, an S3 bucket, a third-party SDK, or a second app to maintain.

Redacted before capture

Blur rules run on the page first, so the user sees the redaction in the share prompt. What you never meant to collect never leaves the browser.

Live help, no backend

Reports land on the webhook you already have. When a report isn't enough, you connect peer-to-peer — no relay, no media server, no bill.

Point at the problem

Your agent sees the customer's screen and draws right on it — spotlight the spot, sketch the fix, or hand over text for them to confirm.

See it happen

    3. They describe it. Redaction runs on the page, before anything is captured or sent.

    Getting started

    It's a script tag. That really is the whole setup.

    Add one line

    <script
    src="/supportlayer.js"
    data-webhook="https://api.domain.com/webhook"
    data-mode="chat"
    data-theme="#14b8a6"
    data-blur-selectors=".sensitive-data, .balance"
    data-fields='[{"name":"issue","type":"textarea",
                  "label":"What went wrong?"}]'
></script>

    Reports POST straight to a webhook you already own. Point data-mode at none for async bug reports, at chat for a live session they type through, or at video for a two-way call. How they reach you is your call on that tag — not a setting the user can flip. Either live mode carries their screen with it, and it stays shared until the session ends.

    Integration guide

    Every attribute, the exact webhook payload, framework recipes and a verification checklist. If you're configuring something, it's documented there — not here.

    Getting your agent in

    Your webhook hands you back a link that is the customer's own page plus two parameters. Open it and you're in the session — no second app, no portal, and no auth of its own to build.

    // what live_session_url looks like
https://app.domain.com/checkout
   ?sl_role=agent&peer=sl-7f3a91

    Your agent gets the customer's screen with a floating dock for pointing, drawing and handing over text — closer to a video call than a dashboard. And whatever already protects /checkout protects this, because it is /checkout.

    That dock and the chat are the entire agent UI. No ticket list, no metrics pane, no separate tool to learn.
